\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\textbf{T1 Construct validity (what we measure).}
Faithfulness is computed by an LLM judge rather than by human annotators
on every answer, so it inherits judge sensitivity---prompt phrasing,
temperature, and the underlying model can all move the
score~\cite{es2023ragas,murugaraj2025ragvue}. The 50-sample human-label
agreement study (Section~\ref{sec:results}) calibrates this risk.
Faithfulness can also diverge from factual correctness: an answer may be
supported by retrieved evidence yet factually wrong, or factually right
yet unsupported by the retrieved context~\cite{murugaraj2025ragvue}.
API-equivalent cost is also a proxy: published provider prices vary by up
to an order of magnitude for the same model, so the GPU-amortised number
is the primary cost result~\cite{elkiran2026empirical}.

\textbf{T2 Internal validity (fairness of the comparison).}
Four risks were addressed before the runs: (i) self-judge---the judge is
now a separate server, enforced by a hard gate; (ii) CRAG proxy
correctness---the flan-t5-base evaluator is not discriminative on
Incorrect passages, so its action distribution is reported with an
explicit caveat rather than treated as ground truth; (iii) data
leakage---eval and corpus are drawn from the same train split by design
(in-domain by construction), so retrieval metrics are not confounded by
missing gold paragraphs; (iv) LLM-as-judge instability---judge prompt and
decoding settings are fixed across cells, and the limitation is reported
rather than hidden~\cite{murugaraj2025ragvue}.

\textbf{T3 External validity (what generalises).}
Results are single-corpus, English-only, single-split (HotpotQA
distractor train), no dense retriever arm, no live web search, and
batch-1 serving under vLLM defaults. HotpotQA is Wikipedia-centric,
which limits transfer to multilingual, domain-specific, and time
sensitive production corpora~\cite{wu2024survey,elkiran2026empirical,zeng2025rare,li2025biomedical}.
Findings do not generalise out-of-domain, to other languages, or to
high-concurrency serving. A small model may also answer from parametric
memory rather than retrieved context.

\textbf{T4 Statistical validity (significance claims).}
$n=300$ per cell gives roughly $\pm 2.9$ percentage points standard
error at a 50\% rate. Paired tests plus Benjamini--Hochberg adjustment
limit false discoveries across the $3 \times 2$ comparisons; effect sizes
should accompany any claim of significance rather than $p$-values alone.

\textbf{T5 System validity (pipeline-specific).}
The fixed chunking strategy, retrieval depth (30 candidates $\rightarrow$
top 10), and embedding assumption are design choices, not neutral
defaults; different depths or chunk sizes can shift both retrieval and
downstream correctness~\cite{elkiran2026empirical}. The local CRAG
proxy also omits the published web-search rescue path and replaces the
trained evaluator with a smaller derivative model, so the crag cells are
a derivative corrective design rather than a full CRAG
reproduction~\cite{yan2024crag}.
