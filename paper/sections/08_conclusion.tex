This paper offers a \textbf{controlled, reproducible benchmark} for
comparing small-model RAG under joint quality, faithfulness, latency,
and cost reporting. It does not claim to establish a new standard or to
settle all production RAG selection questions; broader questions of
domain transfer, dynamic corpora, and retrieval-policy adaptation remain
open~\cite{brown2025systematic,wu2024survey,sharma2025survey,li2025biomedical}.

\textbf{Limitations.}
The grid compares whole pipelines rather than isolating CRAG
components; the corrective stage bundles evaluation, strip editing, and
evaluator latency. The retriever is fixed, so no claim is made about
dense vs.\ sparse retrieval. Costs depend on provider pricing at a
single verification date, and the data is English-only, single-split
HotpotQA distractor. The corrective pipeline is a derivative design, not
a full CRAG reproduction.

\textbf{Future work.}
Add a dense retrieval arm to quantify its recall ceiling; try
alternative judges to bound judge sensitivity; extend to multilingual
small-model subsets; evaluate batched serving rather than batch-1;
sweep AWQ vs.\ fp16 serving settings; and test on time-sensitive or
domain-specific corpora to probe external validity~\cite{zeng2025rare,li2025biomedical,almohaimeed2026arabic}.
